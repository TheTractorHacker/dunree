It answers A and AAAA queries (plus minimal NS and SOA records) for the host names and aliases of your proxy rules, so clients on your network resolve your public names straight to this proxy. Wildcard rules such as *.example.com answer every matching name. It listens on UDP and TCP on one specific address (default port 5353; port 53 needs extra privileges). Per client rate limiting, response size limits and a TCP connection cap are always on. Off by default.
