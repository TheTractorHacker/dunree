Your networks

Name the three network segments (VLANs) and say which address ranges belong to each. Leave a segment empty if you do not have it.

Proxy segmentWhere this Dunree and other proxies live.
Management segmentWhere you administer things from.
Application segmentWhere the servers behind Dunree live.

One network per line, as a range (10.0.2.0/24) or a single address. Names may use letters, digits, spaces and . _ - ( ). "Everything" (0.0.0.0/0) is refused.

Firewall recommendations

A text document written from the saved plan, for UniFi (zone-based firewall) and Proxmox. Nothing is applied and nothing was tested against your network: review it before you use it.

Uses the saved plan, so save your edits first. It contains your networks and the backend addresses of your proxy rules, no passwords, keys or paths.
