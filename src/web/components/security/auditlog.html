What is recorded, and where it is keptSecrets are never written. The file, its size and what the tamper check can prove.
  • Passwords, tokens, cookies and private keys are never written.
  • Kept in conf/security_audit.log (5 files of 5 MiB). It is not part of configuration exports.
  • Addresses are those of the direct connection, not of forwarded headers.
  • The tamper check proves that no entry was edited or removed. A rewrite of the whole file cannot be detected from here.
  • Sign-ins and account changes are listed under Admin accounts.
