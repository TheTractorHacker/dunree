Where Zoraxy sits in your network

The default, Standalone, changes nothing. The other roles are for a chain of two proxies (internet, external proxy, internal proxy, servers) and handle client address headers more strictly. Zoraxy does not change your firewall.

Peer gate

For the internal proxy: refuse everyone except the external proxy and networks you list. The admin panel is not affected.
