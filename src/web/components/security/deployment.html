Deployment

Tell Zoraxy where it sits in your network. The default, Standalone, changes nothing: Zoraxy behaves exactly as before. The other roles are for a two proxy chain (internet → external proxy → internal proxy → servers). They turn on stricter handling of the client address headers and, on the internal proxy, an optional "known peers only" gate. This does not configure your firewall; keep your firewall rules, this is a second layer.

Always on in the External and Internal roles. A connection that is not from a trusted proxy loses its X-Forwarded-For, Forwarded, X-Real-IP, CF-Connecting-IP(v6), Fastly-Client-IP, True-Client-IP, X-Client-IP, X-Forwarded-Host/Proto/Port and X-Client-Cert-* headers. From a trusted proxy, the client is the right-most address in X-Forwarded-For that is not itself a trusted proxy. List your CDN or external proxy under Trusted Proxies first, or every visitor will look like a direct client.

Peer gate (internal role)
