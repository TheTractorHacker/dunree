Role of this Dunree

Most installations are Standalone. The other two are for a chain of two proxies, where the outer one faces the internet and the inner one sits next to your servers.

Client address headers

A visitor can write X-Forwarded-For and similar headers themselves to fake their address. Hardening removes them unless they come from a proxy you trust.

Peer gate

For the internal proxy: refuse everyone except the external proxy and networks you list. The admin panel is not affected.

One IP address or range (CIDR) per line, for example your management network or monitoring host. 0.0.0.0/0 is refused.
